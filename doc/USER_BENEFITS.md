# How AIverse Can Help People

AIverse aims to make AI discovery easier by giving visitors one place to explore tools and understand their possible uses.

- **Students:** discover learning, language, writing and study-support tools. Verify answers and follow academic-integrity rules.
- **Job seekers:** explore tools for CV drafting, proofreading, interview practice and presentations. Personalise all generated output.
- **Creators:** discover tools for brainstorming and creative workflows such as image, audio, video and writing.
- **Professionals:** explore tools that may assist with research, communication, planning and repetitive work.
- **Small businesses:** investigate productivity tools while considering cost, reliability, data protection and terms.
- **Beginners:** learn what different AI tools are designed to do without knowing every product name in advance.

The value is easier discovery and more informed comparison. AIverse does not guarantee third-party services are accurate, free, safe or suitable. Check official provider information.
